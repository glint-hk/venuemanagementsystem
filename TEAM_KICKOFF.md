# Venue Booking System — we're ready to start

Repo: https://github.com/glint-hk/venuemanagementsystem

## Your first 15 minutes

1. Read `ONBOARDING.md` at the repo root — it's short, tells you exactly how we work and what to read next.
2. Follow `docs/SETUP.md` to get it running on your own laptop (Mac or Windows) — Postgres install, env setup, verification steps.
3. Read `AGENTS.md` in full — the 7 non-negotiable rules. Know these on sight.

## Then find your lane

- Your team's prompts are in `Team_Prompt_Playbook.md`, labeled by team and numbered.
- Every story already has a spec in `specs/` — **don't run a prompt for a story until you've read its spec.**
- Ownership map is in `README.md` if you're not sure which folder is yours.

## Two callouts before you build

- `specs/venue-registry.md` and `specs/approval-chains.md` are still TODO — need real IIML data from stakeholders. Analysts, that's you, priority one.
- 7 specs have a `## Contract note` flagging something missing from `shared/` — check yours before assuming a field/shape exists.

Blocked on something crossing team lines → `docs/blockers.md`, raised at morning sync. Need a field that's not in `shared/`? Don't add it — flag it for Architect review.

Questions → this channel.
